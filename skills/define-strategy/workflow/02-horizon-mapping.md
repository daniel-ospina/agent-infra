# Step 2 — Horizon Mapping

Map the gaps into Three Horizons.

- H1 (now): urgent gaps, must resolve immediately
- H2 (build): capabilities to develop, infrastructure to build
- H3 (future): directional bets, long-term vision

**Ponytail check:** Which H2/H3 items can be deferred indefinitely? Which gaps are better accepted than filled? The best infrastructure is unbuilt.

**Output:** Horizons table with gaps assigned to H1/H2/H3.

**AI Review Gate:** Dispatch reviewer. Check: are Horizon assignments realistic? Are H1 items truly urgent? Are there items that should be accepted as gaps rather than filled?

**Human Review Gate:** Present Horizons for approval. Does the sequencing feel right? Human may reorder.

**Commit:** Update strategy doc with Horizons.
