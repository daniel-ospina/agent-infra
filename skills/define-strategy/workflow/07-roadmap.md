# Step 7 — Roadmap

From the strategy doc, produce the Roadmap. The Roadmap is the handoff artifact to the Coordinator.

- H1 items first, with owners and timelines
- H2 items next
- H3 items last (directional, not yet scheduled)
- One-off items get deadlines. Ongoing items get cadence.

**AI Review Gate:** Check: is every initiative in the Roadmap? Are owners assigned? Are timelines realistic?

**Human Review Gate:** Human approves the Roadmap.

**Handoff:** Push Roadmap to Coordinator. The Coordinator now executes.

**Commit:** Final Roadmap at `management/change (S3)/<name>-roadmap.md`.
