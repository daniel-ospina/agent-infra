# Pose & Expression Templates

Different slides need different vibes. Select the template that matches the slide purpose.

## portada-listening

**Use for:** Approachable, listening — best for portada (slide 1). Warm and inviting, draws reader in.

**Expression:** comfortable, at ease. Like listening to someone across the table tell an interesting story. Slight natural warmth. Mouth naturally closed or very subtly upturned. No tension.

**Pose:** seated at table, relaxed posture, facing slightly toward camera

---

## historia-thoughtful

**Use for:** Thoughtful, reflective — best for personal story slides. Shows depth, "I've been through this".

**Expression:** thoughtful, slightly reflective. Looking slightly down or to the side, mid-thought. Not sad — contemplative. Like remembering something important.

**Pose:** seated, one hand resting on table, gaze slightly off-camera to the left, mid-reflection

---

## cta-warm

**Use for:** Warm smile, inviting — best for CTA/final slide. "Come talk to me."

**Expression:** genuine warm smile, eyes slightly crinkled at corners. Looking directly at camera. Inviting, open, "I want to help you." Not a sales smile — real warmth.

**Pose:** seated, leaning slightly forward, open body language, looking at camera with warmth

---

## explaining

**Use for:** Mid-explanation, gesturing — best for insight/framework slides. "Here's what I learned."

**Expression:** engaged, explaining something important. Slight intensity in the eyes — "this matters." Mouth slightly open mid-sentence. One hand slightly raised in a natural gesture.

**Pose:** seated, one hand gesturing naturally as if explaining, body leaning slightly forward, engaged
