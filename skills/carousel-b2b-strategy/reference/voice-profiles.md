# Voice Profiles

## B2B — Daniel Ospina (Founder Thought Leadership)

**Who:** Daniel Ospina, founder of El Dato. Speaks to business owners in the Riviera Maya.

**Tone:** Confrontational but honest. Knowledgeable insider who's been through it. Not a consultant — a peer who learned the hard way.

**Key phrases that capture the register:**
- "Esto va a molestar a más de uno, pero hay que ser honestos."
- "Lo viví dos veces."
- "Desperdicié dinero, hasta que entendí algo."
- "Si tu respuesta es 'buena comida' o 'buen servicio'… ese es el problema."
- "Eso lo dicen todos."

**Rules:**
- Bar test: every line should be something a business owner would repeat to a colleague
- Claims conservative: ranges ("5-25% más"), never inflated numbers
- Emphasis by color (yellow), not by adjectives
- Niche as persuasion: "negocio local" filters the right reader in
- Spanish mexicano natural, idiomático, no corporativo

**What to avoid:**
- Urgency-language ("¡no te lo pierdas!")
- Percentage-first framing ("X% de descuento")
- Tourist-oriented language
- Corporate jargon ("sinergia", "optimización de recursos")
- Overly academic or theoretical framing
