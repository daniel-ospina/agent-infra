# Copy Playbook — B2B Thought Leadership

## Voice Principles

1. **Confrontational but honest.** "Esto va a molestar a más de uno, pero hay que ser honestos."
2. **Bar test.** Every line should be something a business owner would repeat to a colleague.
3. **Claims conservative.** Ranges ("5-25% más"), never inflated numbers. If a number isn't defendable when asked "¿de dónde sale?" — don't use it.
4. **Emphasis by color, not adjectives.** The keyword goes in yellow. Don't underline it with more words.
5. **Niche as persuasion.** "Negocio local" filters the right reader in. It's not limiting — it's targeting.

## Caption Rules

- First line = gancho (visible before "...más"). Repeat the tesis from the portada.
- Brief development + CTA + question that invites comments (comments push the algorithm).
- Brand mention subtle near the close — this is thought leadership, not advertising.
- 5 hashtags max: niche-focused (#negocioslocales, #restaurantesmexico). Avoid saturated giants.

## Slide Copy Patterns

### Portada (Slide 1)
- Hook + promise. "X no funciona — te explico por qué Y qué sí."
- Don't just provoke — promise concrete value.

### La Trampa (Slide 2-3)
- The cost. "$5,000, $10,000 al mes. Los seguidores suben. ¿Pero cuántos visitan tu negocio?"
- Numbers make it real. Questions make it personal.

### Historia Personal (Slide 3-4)
- Credibility. "Mi familia tiene una farmacia. Cada mes pagábamos una agencia."
- Specific and personal. Not testimonial — lived experience.

### El Insight (Slide 5)
- The framework. "Cualquier negocio local crece con los mismos N pilares."
- Declare the niche. Name the pillars.

### Los Pilares (Slides 6-8)
- One per slide. Pilar tag (number + name) + headline + body + emphasis.
- Body explains the problem. Emphasis drives the point home in yellow.

### El Veredicto (Slide 9)
- Why the current approach fails. Ties back to the portada.
- "Por eso las agencias comunes no resuelven tu marketing."

### CTA (Slide 10)
- Autodiagnóstico. "¿Cuántos de los N pilares tiene tu negocio hoy?"
- Link. "Descúbrelo → Link en bio"

## Slide Types

### Photo-Hero (Type A)
- Photo full-bleed. Text overlay on bottom half. Byline top-left.
- Headline: Outfit 700, large, white. Subtitle: Inter, cream.
- Emphasis words in yellow (oblique transform: skewX(-9deg)).

### Text-Slide (Type B)
- Solid purple/deep background. No photo. Lots of negative space.
- Eyebrow: Inter 700, uppercase, yellow, tracking .2em.
- Body: Inter or Outfit depending on content weight.
- Emphasis in yellow.
