# Quote Slide Template

**Usage:** Testimonials, key insights, vision statements, memorable takeaways.

**Markdown:**
```
# [CONTEXT / SECTION LABEL]

> "[QUOTE TEXT — max 150 characters]"

**— [Attribution Name], [Title/Role]**
```

**Brand application:**
- Section label: Outfit 18pt, muted (#C9BBE0), top-left
- Quote text: Outfit 32pt, deep purple (#3F2766), centered
- Quote marks: large decorative, yellow (#F2C94C)
- Attribution: Inter 20pt, purple (#5B3B8C)
- Background: cream (#EFE9DC) with subtle left accent bar in purple

**Example:**
```
# Testimonio

> "El Dato nos trajo 40% más clientes en el primer mes. La mejor inversión que hicimos este año."

**— María García, Dueña de La Cueva del Chango**
```

**⚠️ Max 150 characters for quote text.** Longer → trim or use two-column with context.
