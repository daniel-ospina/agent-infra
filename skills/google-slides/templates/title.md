# Title Slide Template

**Usage:** First slide of every presentation. Brand logo, title, subtitle, date.

**Markdown:**
```
# [PRESENTATION TITLE]

## [Subtitle / Tagline]

**[Author / Department] | [Date]**
```

**Brand application:**
- Title: Outfit 48pt, white (#FFFFFF) on purple (#5B3B8C) background
- Subtitle: Outfit 28pt, muted (#C9BBE0)
- Meta line: Inter 16pt, cream (#EFE9DC)
- Background: purple (#5B3B8C) or deep purple (#3F2766)
- Logo: top-right corner, 120px from top, 80px from right

**Example:**
```
# El Dato Q3 Growth Strategy

## Expansión Regional y Nuevos Segmentos

**Daniel Ospina | Julio 2026**
```
