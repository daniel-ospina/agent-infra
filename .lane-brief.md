# LANE BRIEF — #3453: one HOME per cell (unify on the durable /home/cmux)

## The failure this prevents
Two HOME conventions on the same box. `eldato-orchestrator.service` (the unit that SPAWNS lanes) uses HOME=/home/cmux + CELL_HOME=/home/cmux, while every other cell service uses HOME=/tmp/cell-home + CELL_HOME=/tmp/cell-home. Measured 2026-11-05: a 17-lane wave produced 0 landings because lanes resolved a read-only PAT from the wrong home and every `git push` returned 403 contents=write, while `gh` reads still worked — so it looked like a token problem.
Second consequence, measured 2026-11-06: /tmp/cell-home is the HOME= of 9 unit files on cell-4 and 7 on cell-3, holds 23 GB on each, and is volatile (lost on reboot). It is the single largest reason those cells cannot raise their runner ceiling: each runner is budgeted 8 GB disk + 20 GB reserve, those cells sit at 20-23 GB free, so `by_disk=1` pins the ceiling at 1.

## Target state
- /home/cmux is the ONE home: HOME, CELL_HOME, ENV_FILE all resolve there, in every unit, in the installer templates, and in the scripts that render them.
- /tmp/cell-home becomes legacy: NOT deleted in this PR. Reclaim only after every unit repoints AND a lane proves push=OK + provider=OK from a clean shell with nothing sourced.
